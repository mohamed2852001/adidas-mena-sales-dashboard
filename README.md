# Adidas MENA Sales Dashboard

An interactive data analytics and sales performance dashboard tracking Adidas retail metrics across key Middle East and North Africa (MENA) markets, including Egypt, Iraq, KSA, Lebanon, and Oman. This project transforms raw retail records into dynamic, visual insights to optimize regional supply chains, marketing, and inventory management.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a holistic view of business health, segmenting sales data into four primary analytical dimensions:

1. **Demographic Insights (Orders based on Gender):** - A breakdown of consumer behavior showing order volumes distributed across Female (514 orders), Male (667 orders), and Other (19 orders) categories.

2. **Geographic Performance (Revenue of Products by Region):**
   - Horizontal revenue bars identifying top-performing countries. Egypt leads regional revenue ($86,028), closely followed by Iraq ($81,725), with KSA, Oman, and Lebanon mapping out the remainder of the market share.

3. **Temporal Trends (Count of Sales in Months):**
   - A historical trend line mapping order counts across a 12-month cycle. It highlights a peak performance in May (127 sales) and September (117 sales), alongside a noticeable contraction heading into November (68 sales).

4. **Product Category Distribution (Revenue and Orders by Category):**
   - Segmented into **Footwear**, **Apparel**, and **Accessories**. 
   - **Footwear** dominates financial performance with a staggering $179,006 in revenue, followed by **Apparel** ($77,378) and **Accessories** ($30,994).
   - Order count indicators assist inventory planners by tracking physical volume alongside financial returns (e.g., 502 footwear orders vs 350 apparel orders).

## 🛠️ Features & Interactivity

The workspace is equipped with native interactive controls (Slicers and Timelines) allowing users to dynamically slice data by:
* **Order Date (Timeline):** Filter performance across multi-year quarters (2023–2025).
* **Store Type:** Drill down into specific channels—*Online, Outlet, Retail, or Wholesale*.
* **Category:** Focus entirely on *Accessories, Apparel, or Footwear*.
* **Region:** Isolate performance metrics for specific territories (e.g., viewing *Egypt* or *KSA* independently).
